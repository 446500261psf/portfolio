## Construction

The mark is a **single continuous ribbon** (not a filled heart silhouette):

1. Bottom tip / fold starts the narrative  
2. Vertical / diagonal body  
3. Left lobe = **closed circular loop** with a round negative hole  
4. Path crosses, forming the fold  
5. Right lobe = open soft arc returning into the fold  

Stroke character:

- Thick, even weight (ribbon, not wire)
- Soft outer curves; tip may be diagonal/cut for fold drama
- Overlap uses a **local darker fold shadow** on the under segment

## Colorways (approved)

|------|------|------------|-----|
| Atmosphere | `#FFFFFF` | Teal→forest vertical gradient | Splash, brand films, marketing |
| Primary Brand | `#FD8F1B` | `#FFFFFF` / light UI | App accent, light marketing |
| Reverse on Brand | `#FFFFFF` | `#FD8F1B` | Badges, solid brand fields |
| Reverse on Dark | `#FFFFFF` | Near-black / deep teal | Dark UI headers |
| Mono | Gray / single ink | Neutral | Constrained print / low-ink |

Do not invent new mark colors (no rainbow, no gradient-filled ribbon for the master mark unless a documented special).

## Clear space & scale

- Clear space ≥ height of the left-loop inner hole (approximate) on all sides
- Minimum size: keep the left hole readable; if the hole collapses, scale up or use a simplified approved mono
- Never place on busy photography without a scrim / blur / solid plate

## Don'ts

- Don’t stretch, rotate past subtle angle, or outline with foreign strokes  
- Don’t fill the left hole  
- Don’t apply a **global** drop shadow that floats the whole heart as a sticker  
- Don’t recolor per-section arbitrarily (e.g. green mark + purple mark in one kit)  
- Don’t combine with competing mascots in the same lockup  
- Don’t put type through the ribbon path
