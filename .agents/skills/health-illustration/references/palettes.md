# Illustration Palettes · v0.2

Source of truth also in Figma: `Illustration · Palettes` (file HUAWEI Health Design System).

**Brand parent:** `health-design-language/references/color.md`  
Illustration may soften brand orange; it must not invent a competing identity chroma (no purple-default UI, no cream+terracotta kit).

## Health · Morandi (higher lightness)

Low saturation · gray undertone · **narrow** gamut (sage / mist / clay). Airy daytime wellness.

| Token | Hex | Role |
|-------|-----|------|
| `bg.canvas` | `#F7F3EE` | Canvas |
| `bg.surface` | `#FCFAF7` | Soft fill / cards in art |
| `ink.primary` | `#5A544E` | Linework / text |
| `ink.secondary` | `#8E877F` | Secondary marks |
| `sage.calm` | `#B7C7B5` | Primary |
| `mist.blue` | `#B5C5CE` | Secondary |
| `clay.rose` | `#D4C2BC` | Warm support |
| `stone.taupe` | `#CDC4BB` | Neutral stone |
| `lilac.dust` | `#C7BFCA` | Soft emotion |
| `accent.peach` | `#D8BFA8` | Desaturated brand echo |
| `deep.forest` | `#7A8A78` | Deep accent (still in-gamut) |
| `line.soft` | `#E6E1DA` | Hairlines |

**Rules**

- ≤ 4–5 colors per image; `sage` + `mist` ≈ 70% of filled area
- Forbidden: neon, pure black `#000`, full brand orange `#FD8F1B`, rainbow packs
- Prefer flat soft fills + ink outlines; light paper grain OK (felt, not noisy)

## Exercise · Kinetic (softened saturation)

Wider hue span · motion · still App-safe (not fluorescent).

| Token | Hex | Role |
|-------|-----|------|
| `energy.orange` | `#E89A45` | Primary energy (must appear ≥1×) |
| `heat.coral` | `#E86B78` | Heart rate / sprint |
| `sun.yellow` | `#E6C35A` | Outdoor highlight |
| `volt.lime` | `#8FCB5A` | Achievement |
| `pulse.teal` | `#3DB8B0` | Aerobic rhythm |
| `sport.blue` | `#5B8FD9` | Route / data motion |
| `flare.violet` | `#7A6FD1` | AI+ bridge only when relevant |
| `ink.dark` | `#1A2028` | Night / deep ground |
| `ink.white` | `#F2F4F7` | Reverse line on dark |
| `surface.light` | `#F4F5F7` | Day exercise ground |
| `surface.mid` | `#252D38` | Deep card in art |
| `trail.gray` | `#8E97A5` | Secondary trails |

**Rules**

- 5–7 colors OK; soft complements allowed (orange↔blue, coral↔teal)
- No Health Morandi dust-pinks as dominant fills
- No neon/fluorescent glow; keep matte / soft flat doodle finish
